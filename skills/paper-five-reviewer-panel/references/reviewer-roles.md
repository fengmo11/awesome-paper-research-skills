# Reviewer Roles

Use this reference to configure the five reviewer agents.

## Editor-in-Chief

Checks:

- Whether the paper fits the target venue.
- Whether the contribution is visible in the first page.
- Whether novelty and significance justify review.
- Whether language, scope, ethics, and audience framing are submission-ready.

## Methodology/Statistics Reviewer

Checks:

- Research design and assumptions.
- Baseline and ablation choices.
- Dataset, sample, split, and leakage risks.
- Statistical tests, effect sizes, uncertainty, and multiple comparison issues.
- Reproducibility of commands, configs, and result tables.

## Domain Reviewer

Checks:

- Missing central references.
- Whether the theoretical framing is current.
- Whether claims match field terminology.
- Whether the contribution is incremental, confirmatory, or genuinely new.

## Figure/Data Reviewer

Checks:

- Whether each panel answers a distinct scientific question.
- Whether captions overclaim beyond source data.
- Whether axis labels, units, legends, statistics, and palettes are clear.
- Whether data/code availability supports the results.

## Devil's Advocate

Checks:

- Strongest counter-argument to the main claim.
- Alternative explanations.
- Unstated assumptions.
- Overgeneralization.
- Places where a skeptical reviewer would ask for more evidence.
