# Review Scorecard

Use this reference to score the manuscript and verify revisions.

## Scores

Use 1 to 5, where 5 is strongest.

| Dimension | Question |
| --- | --- |
| Novelty | Is the contribution meaningfully new for the target venue? |
| Significance | Would the target audience care? |
| Evidence | Do results support the central claims? |
| Method | Are design, statistics, and baselines credible? |
| Clarity | Can readers follow the argument and evidence path? |
| Figures | Do figures clarify rather than decorate? |
| Citations | Are key claims supported by suitable sources? |
| Reproducibility | Could another researcher inspect data/code/analysis choices? |
| Venue fit | Does the paper match the venue's scope and expectations? |

## Re-review Criteria

Before submission:

1. Every major comment has a stable ID and action.
2. Every promised revision maps to a manuscript location.
3. No new claim was added without citation or evidence.
4. New figures or analyses have source data.
5. Remaining limitations are explicit.
6. The final decision label is Accept-ready or Minor revision.
